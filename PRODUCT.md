## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: music supervisors and anyone sourcing and licensing music for film, television, commercials, and other media projects.

Secondary: international music artists looking for a close, collaborative place to develop work and have it showcased to the right people for sync opportunities.

## Product Purpose

College Place Audio helps music supervisors and creative teams find and license meaningful music with a story. It also gives music artists a small collective in which to collaborate and a path for their work to be showcased for sync opportunities.

## Positioning

College Place Audio is both a sync agency and an artist collective. Its catalogue is built through close relationships with artists, allowing music supervisors to discover music with context, character, and a story behind it rather than searching an anonymous library.

## Operating Context

The company began four years ago in a small garage studio tucked into a back alleyway. It now has studios in Portugal and Toronto. Its work centres on sound design, music creation, sync representation, catalogue listening, briefs, and music licensing for media projects.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript site.
- About, Work, Contact, and private Portal experiences use slide-in panels.
- Do not invent placements, collaborators, testimonials, metrics, or roster names.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: College Place Audio.
- Tagline: “Make the moment heard.”
- Original framed waveform mark.
- Voice: direct, selective, collaborative, artist-centred, and rooted in meaningful music with a story.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing site and identity assets in `assets/`.
- Selected-work cards already present in `index.html`.
- No approved artist roster, testimonials, or historical photography has been supplied.

## Product Principles

- Keep the collective intimate and human.
- Make the music-supervisor and licensing audience primary.
- Make artist collaboration the reason the catalogue has depth, not a separate design or art service.
- Showcase artists’ work to the right people with clarity and care.
- Keep sound design, music, sync, and licensing at the centre of every message.
- Let the origin story communicate character without overstating scale.
