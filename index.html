College Place Audio is a sync agency and artist collective helping music supervisors and creative teams find meaningful music for film, television, commercials, and other media.

Toronto ↔ Portugal

Four years ago
A garage studio
A back alleyway

Built close to the music.

College Place Audio began in a garage studio tucked into a back alleyway. It was a place for artists to bring unfinished ideas, work together, and make music and sound that meant something.

Four years later, that same spirit connects studios in Portugal and Toronto. The collective remains intentionally small so the company can know the artists, understand the work, and present it to the right people without losing the story behind it.

Start with the picture.

Bring a brief, a rough cut, a reference track, or simply the feeling a scene needs. We help narrow the search to music with a point of view and a real artist behind it.

Bring us the brief.

Tell us about the story, tone, pace, audience, or moment you are trying to create. We listen to the project before reaching for the catalogue.

Find music that means something.

Explore focused selections from artists whose music carries context, character, and a story—not an endless folder of tracks without a point of view.

Move toward the placement.

From discovery through licensing, College Place Audio keeps the process direct and helps the right music reach the project it belongs in.

Made with artists. Heard by the right people.

For artists, CPA is a place to create, collaborate, and develop music and sound design with other artists. More importantly, it is a place for that work to be represented with care and showcased to music supervisors and teams looking for music to license.

The two sides strengthen each other: supervisors discover music with depth and a human story; artists gain a thoughtful route from the studio to film, television, commercials, and other media.

The right music does more than fill a scene.

It makes the moment heard.
